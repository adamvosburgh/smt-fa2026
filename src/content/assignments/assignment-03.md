### Due: 10/1

### What

Take one of the datasets from your Assignment 2 map and imagine a rule you could run on it through time: a forecast, a hindcast, or a what-if. Sketch the interface of the thing that would run it. No code.

The question behind the assignment is what you would have to assume to make your data say something about a time it does not cover.

### The sketch should show

- **The clock.** What steps forward (years? hours? something else?), from when to when.
- **The picture.** What's on the map or the chart, and what changes as the clock moves.
- **The levers.** Three to six controls. For each, a range, a default, and where the default comes from (a source, or you).
- **The rule.** In a sentence or two, or a diagram: what happens to each thing in your data at every step.
- **What it can't see.** A short list of what the data and the rule leave out. This is required.

Hand drawing, diagram, collage, annotated screenshot - anything that fits on one or two pages. The [street tree wireframe](/tutorials/04-notebook-to-sandbox/) in Tutorial 4 and the control panels of the [sandboxes](/sandboxes/) are examples of the content, not of the style.

### Either way, submit:

- the sketch, as a PNG or PDF (one or two pages)
- a two-sentence gallery text
- in the description: which dataset, and 100 words on what your forecast assumes
